Questions, answered

How early should I order a cake?

For standard designs, order at least 2 days ahead. For custom themes, tiered cakes or festive weekends, please give us 4 to 5 days.

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes. Just mention eggless when you place the order and we will bake it that way.

Can I change the flavour, colours or message?

Yes. Pick a cake you like, then tell us the flavour, colours and message on WhatsApp. We will confirm everything before baking.

How does payment work?

We confirm the final price on WhatsApp. Custom cakes are booked with an advance payment, and the balance is paid before delivery.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery charges depend on your location and the delivery slot, and we share them when we confirm your order. Self pickup is also possible.

How should I store my cake?

Keep it refrigerated, take it out 30 to 45 minutes before serving, and keep it away from heat. Fresh cream cakes are best within 24 hours.

Can I return baking supplies?

If your tool arrives damaged or incorrect, message us within 24 hours with a photo and we will make it right. Cakes are made fresh to order and cannot be returned.
